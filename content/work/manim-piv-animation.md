## How it was made

Built in Manim, around four minutes long, walking through particle seeding, interrogation windows, cross-correlation, and the final reconstructed vector field.

**Key parameters**

`tool`: Manim
`length`: ~4 minutes

## Why

I used this to guest-lecture juniors in their sophomore Instrumentation Systems course: an attempt to explain a technique I use experimentally in a way that would land for someone who hasn't touched a wind tunnel yet. It's also the first piece built with an eventual CFD/aerospace YouTube channel in mind, though I haven't uploaded it anywhere public yet since it's not at the quality bar I want.
