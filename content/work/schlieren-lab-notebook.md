## What this is

A running collection of schlieren and shadowgraph shots taken while building the RSD facility for [my thesis](case-file.html?slug=compressible-wall-jet-rsd&category=research) and generally messing around with the setup afterward — this is the "why I actually love fluid dynamics" page as much as it's a lab record. Everything here was shot with a Chronus 2.1HD high-speed camera on a single- or twin-mirror schlieren rig at the Flow Visualization Lab, IOE Pulchowk Campus.

**Key parameters**

`camera`: Chronus 2.1HD, high-speed
`setups`: single-mirror schlieren, Z-type twin-mirror schlieren
`subjects`: candle plumes, salt-water plumes, glycerin diffusion, electric sparks, RSD filter development

## REPLACE ME: candle plume and thermal plumes

A knife-edge schlieren shot of a candle flame and its rising thermal plume — the first thing I visualized when getting the rig aligned, and still the fastest way to check a new setup is working.

## REPLACE ME: salt-water and glycerin diffusion

A salt-water ring diffusing in a water tank, and a separate glycerin-concentration gradient, both imaged to check the rig's sensitivity to weaker (non-thermal) density gradients before trusting it on air.

## REPLACE ME: electric spark

A single electric spark's shockwave and thermal wake, useful as a fast, repeatable transient event for checking camera timing and trigger sync.

## REPLACE ME: RSD filter development

Side-by-side knife-edge and Rainbow Schlieren images of the same flow, showing what the graded color filter actually buys you over a standard knife-edge cutoff.
