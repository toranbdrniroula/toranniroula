## Motivation

Flight controllers cost fortune and they teach very less about low level concepts on flight platforms. Many flight controllers are complex to understand from ground up and present them as a platform to perform high level tasks such as autonomous flights, flight stabilization, waypoint navigation, etc very easily. ESP drone is an opensouce drone platform build upon opensource microcontroller ESP 32. ESP 32 is cheap and easily available. Starting from a basic micro controller platform can help learn so much about how each component is contributing to stable flight. 

---

## Problem Definition

My first objective is to build a stable drone platform which can be further expanded upon needs. The first iteration will be just a stable drone built from scratch with readily available parts and sensors. After building a basic stable autonomous drone we will move to using that drone platform to implement navigation, localization, and mapping with sparse sensors and data.
